we must deconstruct and isolate skills , that mean we must learn how to break a complex skill into a small part and focus on each one of them at a time

# Deconstruct 
break something complex into smaller parts,instead of trying to learn everything at once ,you need to divide a skill into smaller easier steps,and each one of them help understand the next part.

# Isolate
separate one specific part so you can focus on it ,
after breaking a skill into smaller parts, choose one part and practice it without worrying about the other parts,and practice a part until you understand it very well, then you can move to another one.

and after that thy come two steps **Practice** and **combine**

# Practice
repeat doing something to improve your ability and understanding , when you learn a new skill you need to use it several time instead of just read about it, when you practice a skill several times , you become more comfortable with it.


# Combine
combine separated  skills together to make a bigger skill, after learning and practicing individual skills, you merge them together to create a more complex skills. 

# The map

Deconstruct → Isolate → Practice → Combine

1. Deconstruct: Break a big skill into smaller parts.
    
2. Isolate: Focus on one part at a time.
    
3. Practice: Repeat exercises to improve that part.
    
4. Combine: Bring the learned parts together to accomplish a bigger goal.
